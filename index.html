National trend · 2011–2021

How did annual hospitalisations change?

Hospitalisations rose from 34,033 in 2011 to 39,505 in 2021, with a dip in 2020.

BITRE national workbook, T1. Victoria and NSW have breaks in their state series from 2012 and June 2017, respectively.

Residential remoteness · 2011 to 2021

Where did hospitalisations change most?

Major Cities recorded the largest percentage increase. Remote areas recorded a decrease.

BITRE national workbook, T4. Remoteness refers to patients’ residence. Other or unknown residence is excluded from this comparison.

Road user groups · 2021

How did road user shares vary by state or territory?

Car occupants had the largest published share in seven jurisdictions. In the ACT, pedal cyclists accounted for 45%.

BITRE state and territory workbook, T2. Shares use the jurisdiction total, which includes other or unknown road users and unpublished counts.

Age groups · 2021

How did the age distribution differ by state or territory?

People aged 40–64 had the largest published share in all eight jurisdictions.

BITRE state and territory workbook, T4. Age bands span different numbers of years.

Hospital bed days · 2021

Which jurisdictions recorded the most bed days per hospitalisation?

Western Australia ranked highest at 6.25 days per hospitalisation, followed by NSW at 6.12.

BITRE state and territory workbook, raw A–F. Average = total bed days ÷ total hospitalisations. Both totals exclude cases involving death in hospital within 30 days.
